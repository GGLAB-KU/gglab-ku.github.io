Paper accepted to [EMNLP 2026](https://2026.emnlp.org/)!

***
Our paper [Are Non-English Papers Reviewed Fairly? Language-of-Study Bias in NLP Peer Reviews]({{ '/assets/pdf/2604.07119v1.pdf' | relative_url }}) has been accepted to the main [EMNLP 2026](https://2026.emnlp.org/) conference! The paper studies language-of-study bias in NLP peer reviews and introduces **LOBSTER**, a human-annotated dataset for detecting this form of bias. You can read the paper on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07119).
